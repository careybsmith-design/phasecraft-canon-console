# THREAD — narrated, step-by-step video training series

## Purpose and teaching approach
Plan **15 lessons, about 97 minutes**, following the Simple User Guide and actual THREAD controls. Teach why, where to click, what to enter, and how to verify the result. No app changes or video generation yet.

Combine original **3Blue1Brown-inspired animated diagrams** with readable THREAD screenshots and ElevenLabs narration—not the creator's voice or branding. Produce one pilot before the full series.

Use one proposed fictional training example: **reduce internal service-request turnaround from nine days to two**. Label it as training, not company facts or demonstrated results. Follow the same records across lessons.

Every lesson: prerequisite → why → demonstrated steps → visible saved/result checkpoint → common mistake/recovery → practice → next lesson. Explain terms in plain language. Introduce specialist tools when needed, not as an opening terminology lecture.

## Curriculum and walkthroughs

| # | Lesson / length | Practical sequence and learning outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **THREAD: purpose and value** · 4 min | Scattered notes → connected evidence. Explain Signal → Mission → Cell/Burst → Framing → confirmed intent → reuse/build → trial → outcome. Show Feed; distinguish a problem, goal, session and proof. |
| 2 | **Find your way around THREAD** · 6 min | Tour every actual menu by purpose: everyday work, delivery/checking and governance. Show contextual access to Signals from Feed/Missions and Value Companion from the compass. Practise choosing the right destination. |
| 3 | **Record and promote a Signal** · 7 min | Record a signal / Record a new signal → Record directly → Save signal → saved reference → reload. Also Typed capture → Complete capture and review signals → Confirm and save signal; explain upload intake. Inspect duplicates. Promote with rationale; contrast unauthorized suggestion. Prove that saving is not promotion. |
| 4 | **Create a measurable Mission** · 7 min | Missions → Source Value Signal → promoted signal. Enter condition, baseline, target, population, boundaries, proof, investment, review date, authority and stop conditions. Choose different Co-Creator and Builder → save → reopen and verify. Resolve empty signal lists or missing participants. |
| 5 | **Value Cells and Value Bursts** · 5 min | Mission → Form Value Cell → eligible participants/agents → Burst duration → Begin Value Burst. Explain the seven phases. Finish with a Substantive output reference and rationale; inspect output. Elapsed time alone is not delivery. |
| 6 | **Prepare a Framing session** · 6 min | Start session → participants/speaker roles → optional mission association → consent → Open capture. Explain self-consent versus attestation. Demonstrate later association and reversal with appropriate authority, preserving confirmations. Mission association is optional; mission creation still requires a promoted signal. |
| 7 | **Capture notes, conversations and artifacts** · 8 min | Typed notes → Live extraction → supported text → screenshot → inspect captures. Demonstrate actual speech/Outlook/Teams only when working; distinguish simulated transcription. Show blocked/held-back content and recovery. Captures produce drafts, not agreed requirements. |
| 8 | **Clarify and confirm intent** · 8 min | Source → draft → review/correct → needs, behaviours, assumptions, questions and decisions. Show authorized Confirm and missing-authority states. Resolve Live prompts, Material items and contradictions → close → inspect completeness and confirmed provenance. Not every confirmed object is a requirement. |
| 9 | **Graph essentials** · 7 min | Mission Constellation → training mission → node details → Focus. Explain nodes, relationship direction, available filters and navigation. Find a need, behaviour and supporting source; connect the visual map to the mission and Evidence. |
| 10 | **Graph investigations** · 8 min | Need Web, Dependency Web, Decomposition Tree and Contradiction Map answer different questions. Use Path Finder and Impact / Blast Radius; introduce Canon Navigator, Nebula and Graph integrity. Explain family/authority/expiration/detector overlays, edge limits and inferred relationships. Impact is not approval. |
| 11 | **Reuse, then prepare a build** · 7 min | Fabric → search → inspect candidate → Record reuse check → reuse/compose/new-build branches. Creation → confirmed behaviours, outcome contract, evaluated template, reuse check, platform → Compile prompt → dispatch/run evidence. Separate compiling, requesting a build and receiving results; label simulation. |
| 12 | **Safe trials and controlled exposure** · 7 min | Shadow evidence → discrepancies → Progressive exposure → required authority → Authorise exposure → rollback/kill-switch controls. Explain independent review, bounds, expiry and stop conditions. A recorded or simulated run is not proof of an executed external trial. |
| 13 | **Measure, explain and generate documents** · 6 min | Mission production evidence → Measurement → Median intent-to-value time and measure families. Interpret Not measured honestly. Evidence → Explain an object → nine questions → traces. Generation → supported sources/template → cited rendition. Documents are not new authority or proof of value. |
| 14 | **Decisions and governance** · 7 min | Human Decision Queue → evidence → implemented response. Canon proposal → challenge window → steward decision. Inspect Authority boundaries, Detection findings, Assurance evidence and Agents connections. A recorded decision does not prove external execution. |
| 15 | **End-to-end recap and practice** · 4 min | Follow the same references from promoted signal through mission, Cell/Burst, consented Framing, confirmed intent, Graph, reuse, build, trial and outcomes. Mark unavailable/simulated stages. Link earlier chapters; finish with practice, Simulation and Value Companion. |

### Suggested viewing paths
- **New user:** 1–2, then 3–10 in order; 15 as a recap.
- **Business Co-Creator / decision owner:** 1–10, 13–15.
- **Builder:** 1–13 and 15; consult 14 for governance.
- **Steward / reviewer:** 1–2, 8–10 and 12–15.
All menu destinations receive an introduction; substantial workflows receive dedicated demonstrations rather than token mentions.

## Screenshots and presentation
- Capture actual controls and populated workflow states from the training build, not design-proposal images. Existing proposals are brand references only.
- Show destination, inputs, submission, saved result, reopened result and relevant failure/authority state. Use close-ups, one highlighted action and readable pauses.
- Inventory images by lesson/step, record reference, version/date. Hide personal identifiers and secrets; keep case references consistent.
- Use an owner-approved training environment with two genuine participants. Agree labels/cleanup before adding records to the main environment; never weaken safeguards or fabricate evidence. Show any current test-mode banner honestly.
- Match THREAD's charcoal, gold, typography and semantic statuses. Use colour plus labels. Roughly two-thirds practical screenshots and one-third diagrams.
- Output: 16:9 1080p narrated MP4s, chapters, captions, transcript and practice checklist per lesson. No distracting music during instructions.

## Accuracy and acceptance
- Correct guide gaps in scripts: missing Cell/Burst, overly strict Framing sequence, simplified consent and delivery claims. Mission association is optional; promoted-signal creation and human authority still apply.
- Verify document/image extraction clearance, speech, Microsoft and build/trial connections when filming. Unsupported documents stay held back.
- A compiled prompt, selected live mode, recorded trial or decision is not proof of external execution or realised value. Label simulations; leave blocked demonstrations explicit.
- Verify narrated actions against UI, claimed saves by reopening, and graph relationships against the same case. Inspect each lesson for readability, clipping, highlighting, audio and caption alignment. Incomplete motion review means a review draft, not final.
- Check coverage of every menu, workflow, prerequisite, success and recovery. Genuine two-person access and external services are production dependencies, not reasons to invent results.
